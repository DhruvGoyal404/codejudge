# Lucky Plates

A city issues licence plates carrying a positive integer. Superstitious drivers call a
plate lucky when two conditions hold: no two neighbouring digits of the number are equal,
and the sum of its digits is divisible by `D`.

How many lucky plates are there among the numbers `L, L + 1, ..., R`?

## Input
One line with `L`, `R` and `D` (1 <= L <= R <= 10^18, 1 <= D <= 180).

## Output
Print the number of lucky plates in `[L, R]`.
