# Anagram Groups

A puzzle magazine collected `n` words. Two words are in the same group when one can be
turned into the other by rearranging its letters (so `listen` and `silent` are grouped
together, and a word is always in its own group). Identical words also share a group.

Print the number of groups and the number of words in the largest group.

## Input
The first line contains `n` (1 <= n <= 100 000).
Each of the next `n` lines contains one word of 1 to 20 lowercase letters.

## Output
Print two integers: the number of groups and the size of the largest group.
